Services


Amazon Product Research

Conducting in-depth Amazon product research using Keepa, SellerAmp (SAS), and SmartScout to analyze sales history, demand, competition, Buy Box trends, and pricing. Identifying potential products based on profitability, ROI, and market performance.

Online Arbitrage Sourcing

Finding profitable products from online retailers by comparing sourcing prices with Amazon listings. Evaluating historical pricing, sales rank, Buy Box ownership, competition, fees, and profitability to identify viable sourcing opportunities.

Wholesale Product Sourcing

Analyzing wholesale supplier catalogs, price lists, and inventory feeds to identify established branded products with Amazon selling potential. Matching supplier products with Amazon listings and evaluating demand, competition, pricing, and profit margins.

Wholesale Supplier Research & Evaluation

Researching and evaluating legitimate U.S.-based wholesale distributors, manufacturers, and suppliers. Assessing business legitimacy, brand availability, wholesale account requirements, pricing structures, Amazon suitability, and supplier restrictions.

Supplier Outreach & RFQ Management

Handling supplier communications, wholesale account applications, RFQs, and pricing follow-ups. Requesting wholesale price lists, product catalogs, inventory feeds, bulk discounts, and clarification of Amazon resale policies and brand restrictions.

Product Catalog & UPC Matching

Matching supplier catalog items with existing Amazon and retail product listings using UPCs, manufacturer part numbers, model numbers, brands, and product specifications. Verifying exact product matches and organizing sourcing data for accurate purchasing decisions.

Brand Research & IP Risk Assessment

Researching and classifying brands based on Amazon selling eligibility, intellectual property concerns, potential restrictions, and resale policies. Organizing brand allowlists, risk assessments, and restricted brand lists to support informed sourcing decisions.

Profitability Analysis & Lead Validation

Evaluating potential sourcing leads through detailed ROI, net profit, profit margin, Amazon fees, sales volume, competition, and historical pricing analysis. Filtering opportunities to prioritize viable products and reduce sourcing risks.

Amazon FBM Logistics & Fulfillment

Supporting Amazon FBM operations through order processing, shipment coordination, customer order data management, and third-party logistics (3PL) tracking. Monitoring fulfillment workflows and ensuring accurate order updates and delivery coordination.
